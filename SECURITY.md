| Version | Supported |

## Reporting a Vulnerability

For security disclosures or to report a vulnerability, please contact: cannibox.bsky@gmail.com.

I will acknowledge receipt of your report within 48 hours. If the vulnerability is confirmed, I will work to provide a fix as quickly as possible. Please include a detailed description of the issue, steps to reproduce it, and any potential impact you have identified.
